Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH) Policy

This policy outlines our commitment to providing a safe and respectful work environment for everyone.

1. Policy Overview

The organization is committed to a zero-tolerance policy towards sexual harassment. This applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors within our premises or during work-related events.
